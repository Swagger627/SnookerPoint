# Snooker Point — Cashier Quick Start

## Sign in
- Enter your username and password (or PIN if set). If you get locked out after repeated wrong
  attempts, ask a manager/owner to clear the lockout.

## Start your day
1. On **Home**, click **Open Shift** and enter your opening cash.
2. Record **Cash In / Out / Expense / Cash Drop** during the day as needed.

## Tables
1. Open **Tables**. Green = available.
2. Click **Start** on a table → choose **Hourly** or **Fixed amount**.
3. Use **Pause/Resume**, **Add product**, or **Transfer** as needed.
4. Click **Finish**, then **Send to checkout**.

## Sales & payment
1. **New Sale** (or a table checkout) opens the cart.
2. Scan a barcode or search and tap a product. Repeated scans add quantity.
3. Optional discount (may need manager approval).
4. **Take Payment** → Cash / EasyPaisa / JazzCash / Bank Transfer, or **split** across methods.
   Cash change is shown for the cash portion only.
5. **Print Receipt** (58 mm or 80 mm). A reprint is clearly marked **REPRINT**.

## Bookings
- **Bookings** → **New booking** for a reservation. On arrival, **Check in** then **Start** to open a table
  session.

## Close your day
- Home → **Close Shift**. Count your cash; the app shows expected vs counted and the variance.

## If the trial has ended
- You’ll be taken to the **Activation** screen. Your data is safe. Ask the owner to activate the licence.
